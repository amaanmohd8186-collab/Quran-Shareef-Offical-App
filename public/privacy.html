Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Welcome to Quran Sharif Official App. Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting your privacy and ensuring that you have a safe experience while using our application.

1. Zero Data Collection

Quran Sharif Official App is designed to be completely private. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No Name, Email, or Phone Number collection.
  • No Location or GPS tracking.
  • No Device ID or Advertising ID tracking.
  • No Usage Analytics or behavioral tracking.

2. No Permissions Required

This application does not require access to any sensitive features of your device. We do not request access to your:

  • Camera or Microphone
  • Contacts or Calendar
  • Photos, Media, or Files
  • Google Drive or Cloud Storage

3. Local Storage

Any preferences you set within the app (such as bookmarks, reading progress, or prayer alarm settings) are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers and remains entirely under your control.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to provide content:

  • Quranic Content: Text and audio are fetched from public Islamic APIs.
  • Live Ziyarat: Live streams are provided via makkahlive.net and madinahlive.net. We do not use YouTube for live streaming to ensure your privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for use by all age groups.

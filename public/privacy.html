Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 10, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Al-Huda ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience.

1. Information Collection and Use

Al-Huda is designed to function with maximum privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (PII) to our servers.

  • Local Storage: Any preferences, bookmarks, or settings you create are stored exclusively on your device using browser local storage.
  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the App without creating an account or providing an email address.

2. AI Assistant (Gemini API)

The AI Assistant feature uses Google's Gemini API. When you send a message to the assistant:

  • Your query is sent directly to Google's servers for processing.
  • We do not store your chat history on our servers.
  • Your interactions are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Location Data (Qibla Finder)

The Qibla Finder feature requires access to your device's location to calculate the direction of the Kaaba.

  • This data is processed locally within your browser.
  • Your location is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App retrieves Quranic text, audio recitations, and Hadith data from public APIs (such as Alquran.cloud). These services may receive standard technical information (like your IP address) necessary to fulfill the data request.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no risk of your personal information being compromised from our backend. We recommend securing your device to protect the data stored locally.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
